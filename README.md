# E-commerce Sales & Profit Analysis 🛍️📈

An interactive data analysis project on the **Sample Superstore** dataset using **Python and Plotly**, uncovering sales and profit trends across time, categories, and customer segments.

## 🎯 Objective
Analyze e-commerce sales and profitability data to identify top-performing categories, seasonal sales patterns, and customer segments to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries Used
- Python
- Pandas — data manipulation
- Plotly Express & Plotly Graph Objects — interactive visualizations

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
1. **Monthly Sales Analysis** — identified peak and low sales months
2. **Sales Analysis by Category** — Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies contribution
3. **Sales Analysis by Sub-Category** — best-selling sub-categories (e.g. Phones)
4. **Monthly Profit Analysis** — profit trends across the year
5. **Profit Analysis by Category** — most and least profitable categories
6. **Profit Analysis by Sub-Category** — highlighted loss-making sub-categories (e.g. Copiers, Tables)
7. **Sales & Profit by Customer Segment** — Consumer, Corporate, Home Office comparison
8. **Sales-to-Profit Ratio Analysis** — efficiency of each customer segment in converting sales into profit

## 🧹 Data Preparation
- Converted `Order Date` and `Ship Date` to proper datetime format
- Engineered new features: `Order Month`, `Order Year`, `Order Day of Week`
- Handled special character encoding (`latin-1`) for clean data load

## 📊 Key Visuals
- Line chart — monthly sales trend
- Donut/pie charts — sales & profit share by category
- Bar charts — sub-category sales and profit breakdown
- Grouped bar chart — sales vs profit by customer segment
